01 / What we stand for

Six principles.
One direction.

The standard we want every product to meet.

01

Ownership should mean control.

Know who holds your money, who can move it, and how to leave. Choose custody with clear consent.

In practice

Explain custody, permissions, and withdrawals upfront.

02

Clarity is part of the product.

Understand costs and risks before acting. Clear language belongs where decisions happen.

In practice

Show fees, risks, and where yield comes from.

03

Build for everyday usefulness.

Get paid. Pay others. See what’s available. Plan ahead. Start with everyday financial work.

In practice

Build around real tasks.

04

Open rails. Accountable decisions.

Make activity inspectable and tools connectable. Explain who can change, pause, or control the system.

In practice

Publish dependencies, admin powers, and material changes.

05

Resilience before reach.

Test assumptions. Set boundaries. Expand when evidence supports it. Trust takes care and limits.

In practice

Set limits. Test failures. Explain exits.

06

Make participation easier.

Make tools understandable without specialist knowledge. Design for different abilities and experience levels.

In practice

Explain eligibility. Offer accessible guidance and support.
