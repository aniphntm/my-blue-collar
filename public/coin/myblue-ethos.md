## Money should move on your terms.

You build the business. Your money tools should put you in control.

Finance should help people build a life. That’s our starting point.

## Six principles. One direction.

1. **Ownership should mean control.** Know who holds your money, who can move it, and how to leave. Choose custody with clear consent. Explain custody, permissions, and withdrawals upfront.
2. **Clarity is part of the product.** Understand costs and risks before acting. Clear language belongs where decisions happen. Show fees, risks, and where yield comes from.
3. **Build for everyday usefulness.** Get paid. Pay others. See what’s available. Plan ahead. Start with everyday financial work. Build around real tasks.
4. **Open rails. Accountable decisions.** Make activity inspectable and tools connectable. Explain who can change, pause, or control the system. Publish dependencies, admin powers, and material changes.
5. **Resilience before reach.** Test assumptions. Set boundaries. Expand when evidence supports it. Trust takes care and limits. Set limits. Test failures. Explain exits.
6. **Make participation easier.** Make tools understandable without specialist knowledge. Design for different abilities and experience levels. Explain eligibility. Offer accessible guidance and support.
