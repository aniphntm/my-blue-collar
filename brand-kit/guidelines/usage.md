# Logo usage

Use the full lowercase wordmark for headers and brand signatures. Use the compact `mb` mark for avatars and app icons. Prefer SVG for scaling and PNG for tools that require raster images.

Preserve proportions and the original rounded strokes. Transparent files contain blue lettering with no background. Use them on white or another light background; use the white-on-blue tile when contrast is insufficient.

Keep at least one stroke width of clear space around the visible lettering. This is starter guidance, not a previously established brand standard.

## Colors

- Light blue: `#3B82F6`
- Deep blue: `#1D4ED8`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

The SVGs define the exact gradient direction and stroke geometry. Gradient blue is the current logo treatment; the preserved legacy assets may use other colors.
