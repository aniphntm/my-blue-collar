The current MyBlue identity: custom rounded lowercase lettering in a blue gradient, drawn as SVG paths (no font needed). From `brand-kit/assets/logos/` on the `codex/brand-kit` branch, copied verbatim.

- `myblue-transparent.svg`: the full wordmark (viewBox 151 × 64), blue gradient strokes, no background. Headers and signatures on **light** grounds only.
- `icon.svg`: the `mb` mark in white on a blue gradient tile (64 × 64, 14px corner). App icons, avatars, and any dark ground.
- `icon-inverse.svg`: the `mb` mark in gradient blue on a white tile.
- `icon-transparent.svg`: the `mb` mark in gradient blue, no background. Light grounds only.

Gradient: `brand-blue-light` `#3b82f6` (top-left) to `brand-blue-deep` `#1d4ed8` (bottom-right). The gradient is part of the file; do not recolor. The files draw their own colors and show through `<img>` only.

Keep one stroke width (5.5 units) of clear space. The gradient's deep end is about 2.8:1 on the dark page ground, so use the tile on dark. The kit calls this guidance "starter guidance, not a previously established brand standard".

The three `mb` files carry `aria-label="MyBlueTrade"`; override it with an `alt` that matches the channel. PNG exports exist in the branch and are not copied here.
